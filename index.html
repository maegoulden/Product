Hi, I'm Mae! I love solving problems for people.

Born in Woking, I spent a lot of my youth travelling the country playing academy football, going on to represent England at U15s and Greece in the U19s Euros! Football taught me so much; feedback, collaboration, striving to improve and always wanting to learn more - qualities that shaped how I approached my next chapter.

Whilst playing semi-professional football, I started my career in inclusion within education, where I saw first-hand how systems either include people or leave them behind. That's the same thinking I now bring as an Intern at the City of London Corporation and a Product Intern at XALT: understand what people actually need, test assumptions, and adjust based on evidence.
