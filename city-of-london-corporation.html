Problem Discovery

Women interested in digital careers face barriers that make career transitions more difficult.

The Women Pivoting to Digital Taskforce's report, Recruiting and Retaining Female Digital Talent: Insights for Employers, explored the experiences of over 1,000 women who had either transitioned into digital roles or were considering doing so. The findings informed the development of the Turn to Tech programme.

Research Findings

Many women are motivated to move into digital careers but face barriers to making the transition.

The course was informed by two reports published by the City of London Corporation's Women Pivoting to Digital Taskforce. Untapped Digital Talent: The £3bn Opportunity explored the UK's digital skills shortage, while Recruiting and Retaining Female Digital Talent: Insights for Employers surveyed 1,013 women to understand the barriers and opportunities shaping digital career transitions.

89%

Women interested in a digital career would reskill if offered the opportunity.

59%

Women who want to career change said it was unclear how to secure a digital role.

32%

Women interested in a digital career have been given the opportunity to reskill within their current organisation.

The Solution

Turn to Tech - The Digital Career Change Course

In response to the challenges identified through the Taskforce's research, the City of London Corporation developed Turn to Tech - a digital career transition programme designed to help women explore digital careers, recognise their transferable skills, build confidence and understand the practical steps towards employment.

My Role

Transforming an early concept into a complete learning experience

I joined the project after the research, course content and initial concept had already been developed.

My role was to review the existing prototype, provide UX feedback and design the learner experience. This included refining the learner journey, structuring the course flow, designing interactive activities and ensuring the content was presented in a clear, engaging and accessible way. As new sections are developed, they are evaluated through usability testing with 15 participants, allowing feedback to shape ongoing iterations throughout the design process.

Current Progress

An iterative design process informed by continuous user testing

Turn to Tech is currently being developed through an iterative design process. While later sections of the course are still being designed, completed modules are already being tested with members of the target audience. Insights from each round of usability testing are used to refine the experience before progressing to the next stage of the course.

Impact

500+ sign ups so far

Pre-launch, the Turn to Tech: The Digital Career Change Course has over 500 sign ups.
